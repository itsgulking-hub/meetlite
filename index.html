4. Eye Check

Each student's own browser checks if they are awake, drowsy, asleep or away. Video is never saved.

  • Only one status word goes to the teacher
  • Works with a normal webcam
  • Live class attention percentage

Features

  • Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) detection with personal calibration
  • Drowsy after 1.5 s and Asleep after 5 s of closed eyes
  • Away when no face is seen for 5 seconds
  • Timeline of each student's state, one block = 5 seconds
  • Live class attention percentage for the teacher
  • Eye check test page with live eye markers
  • No recording and no images stored

Security & Protection

You stay in charge of your classroom. Decide how every new class starts, and control each student while the class is running.

Rules for every new class

These are saved on this computer and used each time you start a class. You can still change any one student during the class.

During a class

  • Mute mic for one student, or Mute all
  • Camera off for one student, or all
  • Block sharing stops a student who is sharing
  • Remove sends a student out. They cannot rejoin with the same name, and their attendance stays in your report
  • Waiting room and a student limit you choose

Privacy

  • The eye check runs inside each student's own browser
  • Only one word is sent to the teacher: Awake, Drowsy, Asleep or Away
  • No student video or picture is stored
  • Passwords are stored only as a salted hash on this device
  • Each student sees only their own numbers

Presentation / Screen sharing

Show your slides, a document or any window to the whole class while you teach.

How to present

  1. Start a class from Meetings and let your students in.
  2. Press Share screen at the bottom of the room.
  3. Choose a tab, a window or your whole screen.
  4. Press Stop sharing in your browser when you are done.

Tips

  • Share one window instead of the whole screen to keep other things private
  • Use Capture page to save the shared page as study material for students
  • You can stop any student who starts sharing from the student list
  • Use Quick test after your slides to check what was understood

Chat & Messaging

Talk with your class while you teach. Chat is inside the class room, in the side panel, so students never leave the lesson.

How it works

  1. Start a class from Meetings and let students in.
  2. Open the Chat tab in the side panel.
  3. Type a message and press Send. Everyone in the class sees it.

You stay in control

  • Turn Students can write off at any moment, and only you can write
  • Set it off for every new class on the Security page
  • Removed students are cut off from the chat
  • A student can send one message every second at most
  • Messages are not saved after the class ends

LearnLite

Record your lectures, send them to students, keep notes and study material, and run test series (entrance, chapter and mock tests), all in one cosy place.

Overview

LearnLite is where you keep your lessons, study materials and test series. Everything is kept on this computer.

Lessons: record or upload

Record a lesson (camera or screen), or upload a video. To record a live class, press Record lecture inside the class. To give a lesson to students, press Download and send the file.

Materials: upload files

Upload notes, PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, images or videos (each up to 300 MB). PDFs, images and videos open in a new tab; other files download.

Test series

Make a series (for example an Entrance test series), add multiple-choice tests to it, take them with a timer, or share a test with the class that is running.
